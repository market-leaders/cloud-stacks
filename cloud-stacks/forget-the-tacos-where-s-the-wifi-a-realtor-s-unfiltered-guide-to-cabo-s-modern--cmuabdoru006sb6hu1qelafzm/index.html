Your Connectivity Options: From Urban Fiber to Off-Grid Satellite

Your internet choice largely depends on your property's location, but excellent options exist for nearly every lifestyle. From a high-rise condo in Medano Beach to a secluded villa on the East Cape, staying connected is a given.

Healthcare: Accredited Hospitals and English-Speaking Doctors

The local healthcare system is a significant draw for expats and retirees. You'll find accredited, state-of-the-art facilities with English-speaking staff ready to handle everything from routine check-ups to emergencies. For detailed information, our guide to healthcare for Americans in Cabo is a must-read.

Key facilities like H+ Los Cabos and the BlueNet Hospitals network are known for their modern equipment and specialties. They often work with US insurance providers and medical evacuation services, ensuring a continuum of care. Beyond major hospitals, the area is rich with excellent dental clinics, specialists, and well-stocked pharmacies (farmacias).

Essential Utilities: Power, Water, and Banking

The backbone of daily life here is solid. The power grid, managed by CFE, is stable in primary residential zones, and many homeowners supplement with solar panels to lower costs and ensure backup power. Water is supplied via municipal sources, desalination plants in newer luxury developments, and reliable water delivery services (pipas) that fill a property’s cistern (pila).

Modern conveniences extend to shopping and banking. You’ll find international banks, Costco, Walmart, and Home Depot, making it easy to furnish your Cabo home and manage your finances just as you would back in the States.

Getting to and around Cabo is seamless, thanks to a modern international airport and well-maintained transportation networks.

A key factor for any second-home owner is accessibility. Los Cabos is exceptionally well-connected, making it easy to pop down for a long weekend or welcome guests from back home. The infrastructure is designed to move people efficiently and safely.

Los Cabos International Airport (SJD)

SJD is a world-class airport that acts as a gateway to paradise. It services direct flights from dozens of major US hubs, including LAX, DFW, PHX, DEN, and JFK. You can leave home in the morning and have your feet in the sand by the afternoon. For the luxury market, several Fixed-Base Operators (FBOs) cater to private aviation, offering discreet and efficient service.

Local Transportation Infrastructure

The four-lane Tourist Corridor highway connects Cabo San Lucas and San Jose del Cabo, making the 20-minute drive between the two towns quick and scenic. While the ride-sharing landscape with Uber and Didi can be fluid, licensed taxis and private Cabo airport transportation services are plentiful and reliable. It's important to set realistic expectations for roads in more remote areas like Zacatitos, where a 4x4 vehicle is often a wise investment—a detail a seasoned realtor will always point out.
