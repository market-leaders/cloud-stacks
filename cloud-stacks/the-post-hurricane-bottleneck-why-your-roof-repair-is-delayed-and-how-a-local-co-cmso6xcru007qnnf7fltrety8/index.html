The Material Scramble: Why Shingles and Underlayment Vanish Overnight

After a storm, every damaged home in the region needs the same things at the same time. National suppliers reroute inventory, but it’s never enough to meet the sudden, overwhelming demand. This leads to a frantic scramble where contractors without established local credit lines and relationships are told they’ll have to wait weeks, or even months, for basic materials needed for an asphalt shingle roof.

The Labor Shortage vs. The "Storm Chaser" Influx

The Gulf South has a finite number of experienced, licensed, and insured roofing crews. Suddenly, they're joined by hundreds of out-of-state "storm chasers." These crews put further strain on material supplies, clog up permitting offices, and often perform shoddy work before disappearing—leaving you with a faulty roof and a voided warranty. This is the exact scenario that the "Shop Local" Skeptic fears most.

The Insurance Adjuster Logjam

Your insurance company is also overwhelmed, flying in adjusters from across the country who are unfamiliar with Louisiana and Mississippi policies or specific storm damage patterns. This creates a huge backlog for claim approvals. A contractor who doesn't know how to properly document damage or communicate effectively with adjusters will see your project stalled for months.
