Step 1: Defining Your "Cash-Flow Criteria"

A good rental property typically has key features that attract quality tenants: 3+ bedrooms, 2+ bathrooms, a good school district, and proximity to major employers. As a quick screening tool, we use the "1% Rule," which suggests the gross monthly rent should be approximately 1% of the purchase price.

Step 3: Running the Numbers Like a Pro

Here’s a simple calculation to estimate your potential monthly cash flow:

(Gross Monthly Rent) - (Mortgage + Taxes + Insurance + 10% for Maintenance + 8% for Property Management) = Monthly Cash Flow

This formula transparently includes the cost of professional property management, a necessary expense for any smart, passive investor.
