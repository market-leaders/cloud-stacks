Instead of clinging to obsolete metrics, it's time to build a new report that reflects reality. The GEO Scorecard shifts the focus from position to presence. It’s not about where you rank; it’s about how often you *are* the answer. This is especially powerful for proving value in local SEO, where user intent is incredibly specific. ### Metric #1: AI Answer Extractability (The New 'Above the Fold') This metric tracks how frequently your client's content is directly cited or featured in AI-generated answers for your target commercial-intent keywords. * **How to Track:** Use tools to monitor AI responses for key queries. Manually check Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT for citations and mentions of your client's content. Document every instance with screenshots. * **Example Goal:** "Increase client citations in AI answers for 'HVAC repair cost' queries by 30% in Q2." ### Metric #2: Knowledge Graph Saturation (Local SEO's New Frontier) This measures the accuracy and completeness of your client's entity information across the web's knowledge graphs, which directly feed AI engines. For a local business, this is non-negotiable. An AI can't recommend your client if it has the wrong phone number or operating hours. * **How to Track:** Audit AI responses for queries like "[Client Name] hours," "[Client Service]," and "[Client Address]." Is the AI's answer 100% correct across multiple platforms? * **Example Goal:** "Achieve 100% accuracy for client NAP and service data in AI answers across Google, Bing, and Perplexity." ### Metric #3: Brand-Term Correlation Strength (The Ultimate Authority Signal) This advanced metric tracks how strongly AI engines associate your client's brand with non-branded, high-value terms. The goal is for the AI to volunteer your client as the solution without being explicitly asked about them. This is the holy grail of Generative Engine Optimization. * **How to Track:** Pose questions like "Who is the most reliable roofer in Phoenix?" or "What company is best for commercial plumbing in Miami?" to various AIs. The goal is to have your client mentioned organically. This requires a deep entity strategy. * **Example Goal:** "Establish a brand-term correlation where 3 out of 5 AI engines mention our client when asked for the 'best [service]'." ## Building a Winning GEO Scorecard Requires an AI-First Content Pipeline Building a winning GEO Scorecard requires an AI-first content pipeline that automates structured data, syndication, and multi-platform distribution.
