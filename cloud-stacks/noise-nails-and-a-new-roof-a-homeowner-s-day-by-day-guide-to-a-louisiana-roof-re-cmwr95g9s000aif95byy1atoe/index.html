What Should I Do to Prepare My Home for a Roof Replacement?

You should prepare your home by clearing the driveway and yard of vehicles and furniture, protecting valuables in the attic, and securing wall hangings. Our Priority Roofing project manager will walk you through a pre-installation checklist to ensure every detail is covered before our crew arrives.

Day 0: The Pre-Installation Walkthrough

Your dedicated project manager will meet with you to confirm the project timeline, dumpster placement, and material delivery. We'll answer any last-minute questions and review access points for our crew. This is our chance to ensure we're aligned on protecting your landscaping, pool, or other specific property features before work begins.

Your Home Prep Checklist

  • Clear the Perimeter: Move cars from the driveway, and relocate patio furniture, grills, potted plants, and children's toys at least 15 feet away from the house. This creates a safe zone for our team and protects your belongings.
  • Protect the Attic: Cover any stored items in your attic with tarps or old sheets to protect them from dust and small debris that can fall through the decking.
  • Secure Your Walls: The vibrations from hammering can sometimes cause pictures or decorations on walls and shelves to shift or fall. Take a moment to secure or temporarily remove any fragile or sentimental items.
  • Plan for Pets & People: The noise can be stressful for pets and disruptive for anyone working from home. We recommend making arrangements for them to be elsewhere during the main workdays for their comfort.

What Actually Happens During a 1- to 3-Day Roof Replacement?

During a roof replacement, our crew systematically protects your property with tarps, tears off all old materials, inspects the wood decking for damage, and professionally installs your complete new roofing system. We follow a strict process designed for the unique weather challenges of the Gulf South, from the River Parishes to the Mississippi Gulf Coast.

Day 1: Tear-Off and Decking Inspection (The Loudest Day)

Our crew arrives in the morning, introduces themselves, and begins protecting your home's exterior, windows, and landscaping with heavy-duty tarps. By mid-day, the tear-off begins. This is the noisiest part of the job as we strip old shingles, felt paper, and nails to get down to the bare wood.

Once the roof deck is exposed, we conduct a thorough inspection for any soft, rotted, or damaged wood—a common issue in Louisiana's humid climate. We will document any necessary repairs with photos and get your approval before proceeding. This step is critical for the long-term health of your entire roofing system.

Day 1-2: Installing Your New Hurricane-Ready Roof

We begin by installing critical waterproofing components like synthetic underlayment and ice & water shield in valleys and around penetrations like pipes and vents. Next comes the metal drip edge and flashing, which are essential for directing water away from your fascia and preventing leaks.

Finally, we install your new shingles according to manufacturer specifications, ensuring a proper seal that can withstand hurricane-force winds. This is where we can integrate a FORTIFIED Roof system, a superior building standard that can help lower your insurance premiums and provide maximum protection for your home.

Daily Cleanup: The Magnetic Sweep

Our commitment to you includes leaving your property as clean as we found it. At the end of each day, we clean up debris and run a high-powered magnetic roller over your yard, driveway, and walkways to collect stray nails. Your family's safety is our top priority.
