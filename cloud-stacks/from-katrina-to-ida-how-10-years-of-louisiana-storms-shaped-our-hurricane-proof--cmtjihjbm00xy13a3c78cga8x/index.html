Hurricane Isaac (2012): The Failure of the Drip Edge and Underlayment

Isaac was a slow-moving storm with relentless, wind-driven rain. We saw firsthand how water was forced *up* and under the shingles at the roof's edge, compromising standard felt paper and rotting the roof decking. This is why a sealed roof deck and high-performance synthetic underlayment became non-negotiable parts of our process. We ensure the roof is waterproof *before* a single shingle is installed.

The 2020 Season (Laura, Delta, Zeta): The Brutal Test of Shingle Fastening

This rapid-fire season showed how repeated high winds exploit the weakest link: the fasteners. We saw entire sections of roofs "unzip" because they were installed with the standard four-nail pattern, a clear sign of wind damage. In response, we adopted a mandatory six-nail, ring-shank nail pattern for every shingle, providing over 50% more wind resistance and meeting a core requirement of the FORTIFIED standard.

For Our Commercial Clients in Baton Rouge & Biloxi: The Vulnerability of Flat Roof Seams

Major storms create massive pressure changes that can lift the seams on large TPO and flat roofs, common on commercial buildings. We saw how one failed seam could lead to catastrophic inventory and operational losses. This reinforced our commitment to meticulous heat-welding on all TPO seams and developing proactive commercial maintenance plans to inspect these vulnerable points *before* storm season hits.

Hurricane Ida was the ultimate test of our storm-hardened process, proving that a system-based approach like the FORTIFIED Home™ standard is the only reliable defense for Gulf South properties.

Hurricane Ida in 2021 was not a disaster for our roofs; it was a validation. The culmination of years of lessons learned, our system-based approach proved its worth against one of the most powerful storms to ever hit Louisiana.

Putting It All Together: Our Hurricane-Proof Installation Checklist

The hard-won knowledge behind Priority Roofing's hurricane-proof process is now a standard checklist for every project. This isn't just our opinion—it's a system proven by Mother Nature.

  • Sealed Roof Deck: We tape all seams to prevent water intrusion even if shingles are lost.
  • Superior Underlayment: We use high-performance synthetic materials, not cheap felt paper.
  • Locked-Down Drip Edge: Special flashing and sealant block wind-driven rain at the most vulnerable point.
  • Ring-Shank Nails: We use six nails per shingle for maximum grip and wind resistance.
  • Wind-Rated Shingles: We install shingles specifically tested and rated for hurricane-force winds.
