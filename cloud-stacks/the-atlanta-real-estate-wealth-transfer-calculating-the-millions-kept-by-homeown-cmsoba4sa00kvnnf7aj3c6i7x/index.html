How the 6% Commission is Actually Split in a Georgia Real Estate Deal

The total commission isn't a single fee paid to one person. It's typically divided equally between the two brokerages involved in the transaction: the listing brokerage (representing the seller) and the buyer's brokerage (representing the buyer). A common point of confusion for homeowners is who pays for what. The seller is responsible for paying the *entire* commission fee out of their sale proceeds at closing.

  • Listing Agent's Brokerage: Receives approximately 3% of the sale price. This is the fee for marketing the home, managing showings, and negotiating on the seller's behalf.
  • Buyer's Agent's Brokerage: Receives approximately 3% of the sale price. This is the fee offered to incentivize agents to bring qualified buyers to the property.

So, on a standard 6% agreement, you are paying your agent's company 3% and the buyer's agent's company 3%.
