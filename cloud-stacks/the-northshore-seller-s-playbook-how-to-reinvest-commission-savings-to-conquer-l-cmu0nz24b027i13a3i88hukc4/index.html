According to the Insurance Information Institute, Louisiana has consistently faced one of the highest average homeowners insurance premiums in the nation, a trend that has only intensified. For a home seller, this means a potential buyer's ability to secure affordable coverage is a primary contingency of the sale. Even the type of plumbing can trigger a denial.
