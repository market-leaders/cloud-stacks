Deconstructing the Traditional 6% Commission in Louisville

For decades, the 6% commission has been the unquestioned standard, but let's break down what that really means for your wallet. It’s not just a small fee; it’s a massive chunk of your home's value that you never see. Consider a home in Louisville that sells for $300,000, a price point familiar to many in the area. A traditional 6% commission on that sale is a staggering **$18,000**. That isn't Monopoly money; it's a direct reduction of the equity you were counting on for your next big step.

How Commission Directly Impacts Your Next Down Payment

Let's connect the dots. That $18,000 isn't just a business expense; it's $18,000 *less* you have for the down payment on your new home. This financial hit can have serious consequences. It could be the difference that forces you to pay Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) for years, increasing your monthly payment. It could shrink your budget, pushing that perfect home in your dream neighborhood just out of reach. Suddenly, one of the most common challenges when selling a home becomes the fee you pay to sell it.
