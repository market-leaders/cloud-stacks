Deconstructing the 6% Commission

For years, the 6% commission has been the default setting in real estate transactions. This figure is typically split down the middle: 3% goes to the brokerage representing the seller (the listing agent) and 3% goes to the brokerage representing the buyer. The fundamental issue with this percentage-based fee is that the more your home is worth, the more you pay in flat fees, even if the work involved remains the same. The effort to sell a home in Signal Mountain isn't necessarily double the effort of selling one in Red Bank, yet the commission often is.

Let's put this into perspective with a tangible local example. On a $400,000 home sale in Chattanooga, a standard 6% commission amounts to a staggering $24,000. That's $12,000 paid to your listing agent's brokerage alone, a significant portion of your home's value that you never see.
