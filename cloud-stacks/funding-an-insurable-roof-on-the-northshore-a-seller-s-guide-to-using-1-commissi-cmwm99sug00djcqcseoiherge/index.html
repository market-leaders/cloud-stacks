Why is it so hard for buyers to get homeowners insurance in Louisiana?

Buyers struggle to get insurance in Louisiana because numerous insurers have left the state, forcing remaining companies to tighten underwriting standards, especially for roofs over 10-15 years old. This trend has been particularly acute following recent hurricane seasons, as noted by industry organizations like the Insurance Information Institute. For sellers, this isn't a distant problem; it's a direct threat to your closing.

The Post-Hurricane Reality for St. Tammany Parish

Insurers are aggressively managing their risk by non-renewing policies and denying new ones based almost entirely on roof age and condition. This directly impacts a seller on the Northshore. A buyer cannot secure a mortgage without proof of homeowners insurance, which means a huge portion of your potential buyers are automatically disqualified before they even make an offer on your home.

What Makes a Roof "Uninsurable" in Today's Market?

An underwriter may deem a roof uninsurable due to several common red flags, even if it has no active leaks. These include the roof's age (often a strict cutoff at 15 years), any visible damage like curled or missing shingles, certain outdated shingle types, and a lack of documentation proving its installation date. A roof that has protected your home perfectly for years can still be the single reason a sale falls apart.

How Does This Affect Your Covington, Mandeville, or Madisonville Home Sale?

The consequences for your home sale are severe and immediate. You can expect fewer offers, and the ones you do receive may be lowball offers from cash buyers who don't need a mortgage and know they have leverage. Your home will likely sit on the market longer, and even if you do accept an offer, the deal is at high risk of collapsing during the inspection and insurance procurement period, a common issue in the current Mandeville housing market.
