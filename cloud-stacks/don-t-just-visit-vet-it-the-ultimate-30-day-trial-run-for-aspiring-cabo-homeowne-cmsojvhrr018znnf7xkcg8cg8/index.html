Why a Vacation Isn't Enough

A 30-day 'trial run' is the single most effective way for American buyers to bridge the gap between a Cabo vacation fantasy and the reality of homeownership. A week at a five-star resort is a perfectly curated experience, engineered to hide the mundane. A month-long stay, on the other hand, is designed to reveal it—and that’s exactly what you want before making a major investment.

Discover Daily Life Beyond the Tourist Zone

This is where the fantasy meets the road. You’ll quickly learn the difference between vacation-mode and resident-mode. The grocery run, for instance, becomes a strategic choice between the familiar aisles of Costco and Walmart versus the more local selections at Fresko or Chedraui Selecto. You'll experience the real rush hour traffic on the Corridor between Cabo San Lucas and San Jose del Cabo and decide if you truly need a car. You'll also get a firsthand look at utility bills from the CFE (the electric company), especially after running the A/C during the warmer months, giving you a real sense of the cost of living in Cabo San Lucas.

Find Your Community's True Vibe

A vacation shows you the nightlife; a trial run shows you the neighborhood. Are you energized by the constant buzz of Medano Beach and the marina in Cabo San Lucas year-round? Or does the quieter, arts-focused historic downtown of San Jose del Cabo feel more like home? Perhaps the manicured exclusivity of a golf community along the Corridor is your speed. Or maybe you’re drawn to the off-the-grid, adventurous spirit of the East Cape in places like Zacatitos, BCS, Mexico. You can’t know until you live it.

Calculate the Real Cost of Paradise

The sticker price of a home is just the beginning. A 30-day stay allows you to track your real-world spending on groceries, dining out at local gems (not just the tourist traps), gas, and entertainment. More importantly, you'll start to understand the ownership costs in Cabo that tourists never see. This includes HOA fees, which can vary dramatically between communities, property management fees if you plan to rent out your home, and the annual bank trust fees for your Fideicomiso.

Your Blueprint: The 30-Day Cabo Vetting Plan

Executing a successful 30-day Cabo trial involves a structured plan focused on logistics, neighborhood exploration, and professional networking. As the local experts in real estate from Cabo San Lucas, BCS, Mexico to the East Cape, BCS, Mexico, the team at Coastal Properties of Cabo has crafted this blueprint based on years of helping clients make this exact transition.

Week 1: Logistics & Settling In

Your objective this week is to get your bearings and establish a "home base." Rent a car to understand the freedom and challenges of driving here. Visit a few different grocery stores and farmers' markets to see what you like. If you work remotely, set up a temporary workspace and rigorously test the internet speed and cell service in your rental. Most importantly, walk your temporary neighborhood at different times of day—morning, noon, and night—to get a feel for its rhythm.

Week 2: Deep Dive into Neighborhoods

Now it’s time to explore potential communities with the discerning eye of a resident. Dedicate full days to specific areas: Downtown Cabo San Lucas, the Tourist Corridor, historic San Jose del Cabo, and take a drive out to the East Cape to feel the difference. Visit a community beach club, have coffee in a local plaza, and just observe the pace of life. This is the perfect time to leverage the insider knowledge from the seasoned experts at Coastal Properties of Cabo with a "neighborhood orientation tour" to uncover hidden gems.

Week 3: The Practicalities of Life

This week is about the "boring" but critical aspects of living in Los Cabos. Visit a local bank like Intercam or CI Banco and ask about the process for a foreigner to open an account. Locate the nearest high-quality hospitals and clinics, such as Hospiten or BlueNet, to understand your healthcare options. Strike up conversations with other expats you meet and ask them what surprised them most about moving here. Attend a local event like the San Jose Art Walk to see the community in action.

Week 4: Building Your Professional Team

In your final week, you'll transition from "vetting the life" to "vetting the purchase." Schedule a no-pressure consultation with a real estate agent to discuss your findings, budget, and non-negotiables. Ask them for referrals to a reputable closing attorney and a cross-border mortgage broker if you're considering financing a home in Cabo. Start reviewing active listings on the MLS that match your preferred neighborhoods and tour a few different property types—a condo, a single-family home—to solidify what you truly want.
