The Anchoring Effect: Is 6% a Rule or Just a Rut?

**Anchoring Effect**: A cognitive bias where an individual depends too heavily on an initial piece of information offered (the "anchor") when making decisions. In real estate, the first number most sellers hear is "6%," and it becomes the benchmark for what feels "right." This 6% figure became standard decades ago, long before the internet, Zillow, and digital marketing streamlined the entire process. It’s a relic from an era of physical "listing books" and limited information access. Today, the work is more efficient, but the anchor of 6% remains, costing sellers dearly in the current Mandeville housing market and beyond.
