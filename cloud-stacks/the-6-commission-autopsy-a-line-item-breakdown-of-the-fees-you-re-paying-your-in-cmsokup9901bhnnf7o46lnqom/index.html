The 3% Listing Side Autopsy: Where Does Your Money Really Go?

  • The Brokerage Split: The first cut. Up to 50% of your agent's 3% fee goes directly to their managing broker (e.g., Century 21, Keller Williams) to cover brand fees and overhead. That's half your fee gone before your agent even starts marketing.
  • The Office & Overhead Tax: A slice for the expensive brick-and-mortar office in Carmel or a high-rise downtown, plus administrative staff and franchise fees. You're paying their rent and utility bills.
  • The Antiquated Marketing Fund: Dollars spent on low-ROI activities like park bench ads, newspaper placements, and mass-printed postcards that are relics of the 1990s. In a digital world, these methods are both costly and ineffective.
  • The Agent's Actual Cut: After all splits and fees are paid to the mothership, the agent you hired sees only a fraction of the 3% you paid them. This structure forces them to charge you more just to make a living.
