The Commission Paradox: Why Your Agent Gets a Pay Raise on Your Equity (For Doing the Same Job)

The "Commission Paradox" occurs when a real estate agent's pay automatically increases due to rising home prices, despite their work remaining the same. A 6% commission on a $500,000 home is $30,000, but on a $700,000 home, it becomes $42,000 for the same services, directly transferring a homeowner's market-gained equity to the agent.
