This led to the establishment of the Multiple Listing Service (MLS). The MLS created a system of "cooperative compensation," where listing brokers would offer a share of their commission to any agent who brought a qualified buyer. Over decades, this structure solidified a standard commission rate around 6%, which became the unquestioned norm in metro Atlanta and across the state. ### The Commission Split Explained: Where Does Your 6% Actually Go? Many sellers mistakenly believe their agent pockets the entire commission. In reality, the fee is typically split four ways.
