Think of your business as an "entity" defined by a set of attributes: Name, Address, Phone (NAP), service areas, hours of operation, professional licenses, and geo-coordinates. Decay happens when these attributes become inconsistent. It's digital rust. * A law firm moves offices, but a dozen legal directories still point to the old address. This confuses location-based AI queries for legal services. * A plumbing company updates its emergency service hours on its website, but Google Business Profile and Yelp disagree. An AI seeking a "24-hour plumber near me" will deem the data unreliable and ignore the company. * A real estate brokerage adds a new agent, but their profile isn't linked correctly to the main brokerage entity, making them invisible in searches for agents in their specific territory. These are not minor clerical errors. They are fractures in your digital foundation. Each inconsistency lowers an AI's confidence in who you are, what you do, and whether you can be trusted enough to recommend to a user. ### Q: How do AI search engines like Google perceive this decay? **A:** AI models, like those powering Google's AI Overviews, are not "searching" in the traditional sense.
