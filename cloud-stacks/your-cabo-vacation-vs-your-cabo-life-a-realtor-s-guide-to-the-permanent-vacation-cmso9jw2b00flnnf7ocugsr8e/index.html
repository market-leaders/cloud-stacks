From Tourist Tab to Monthly Budget

That open bar tab at the resort is a distant memory. As a homeowner, your budget includes real-world expenses. We're talking about CFE (electricity), water, high-speed internet, annual property taxes, and the all-important Fideicomiso (bank trust) fees. Understanding the true cost of living in Cabo San Lucas is the first step to financial peace of mind in paradise.

The True Cost of Paradise

The costs of ownership in Cabo vary significantly by community. You might be weighing the HOA fees in a prestigious community like Pedregal or a golf community like Cabo del Sol against the maintenance costs of a standalone villa. Each has its pros and cons, from security and amenities to freedom and privacy. We help you analyze these costs to find the perfect fit for your budget and lifestyle.

Setting Up Your Life

There are a few essential steps to becoming a resident. This includes opening a Mexican bank account, obtaining your Residente Temporal or Permanente visa, and finding reliable healthcare providers. These processes are straightforward but require guidance, which is a core part of the service we provide to our clients moving to Cabo San Lucas.

The Pace of "Mañana"

One of the biggest adjustments is embracing the relaxed pace of life. Things may take longer than you're used to back home, whether you're at the bank or waiting for a handyman. Patience is a virtue here, and learning to slow down is one of the greatest rewards of living in Los Cabos. It's not a bug; it's a feature.
