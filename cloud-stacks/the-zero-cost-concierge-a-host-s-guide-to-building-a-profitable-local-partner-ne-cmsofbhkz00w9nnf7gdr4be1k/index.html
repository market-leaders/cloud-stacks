The How — Your Step-by-Step Action Plan

Building a profitable local partner network starts with identifying businesses that align with your guest profile and proposing a simple, mutually beneficial commission-based relationship.

Step 1: Identify Your Ideal Partners (The "Guest Journey" Method)

Map out a typical guest's day at your property to identify potential partners. Don't just think about your city; think about your specific neighborhood and what makes it convenient and special for a traveler.

  • Morning: The independent coffee shop with the best pastries, a local yoga studio, or a breakfast spot known for its pancakes.
  • Afternoon: Bike or kayak rentals for exploring the area, a guided tour like a historical walking tour of Savannah, a local brewery in Denver, or family attractions.
  • Evening: The best restaurants for a nice dinner, a casual pizza place that delivers, or even a private chef service for a special occasion.
  • Services: Airport shuttles, luggage storage, ski or surf gear rental, and grocery delivery services that can stock the fridge before guests arrive.

A great way to find these businesses is to search online for terms like "bike rental near me" or "best coffee in 80202" to see what guests will find. Prioritize businesses that are highly rated and convenient to your property.

Step 2: The Approach: How to Pitch a Win-Win Partnership

Reaching out to a local business owner is simple. You are offering them something of real value: free, targeted marketing to a steady stream of new customers. You can call, email, or even stop by in person during a slow period.

Here's a simple script you can adapt:

"Hi, my name is [Your Name]. I own and operate a popular short-term rental just a few blocks from here, and I'm building a curated guide of my favorite local spots for the hundreds of guests I host each year. I'd love to feature your business. In return for sending you qualified customers, I'm proposing a simple 10-15% referral commission on the business I send your way."

Most local business owners will be receptive to a proposal that brings them more customers with no upfront cost. It's a true win-win.

Step 3: Structuring the Deal: Tracking Referrals for Profit

Once you have a verbal agreement, you need a simple way to track the referrals. In the beginning, you don't need complicated software. Here are a few low-tech methods that work:

  • Promo Codes: Ask the business to create a unique code for your guests, like "PROPERTYNAME10," that gives them a small discount and tracks your referrals.
  • Physical Cards: Create simple, branded business cards for your property that guests can present to the partner business to claim an offer.
  • QR Codes: Generate a unique QR code that links to the partner's booking page with an affiliate tracker embedded in the URL.

While these methods work, they require manual effort to track sales and chase down commission payments. This administrative hassle is precisely the problem that the owner-first tools from ASTRO are designed to solve.
