Your First 48 Hours: A Calm, Step-by-Step Guide to Post-Storm Action

Our proven process begins by helping you secure your property and prevent further damage, establishing a clear path forward from the moment you call.

Step 1: Prioritize Safety Above All Else

Before you even think about your roof, address immediate dangers like downed power lines, unstable trees, or visible structural damage. It is crucial that you stay off the roof yourself. A storm-damaged roof is unstable, and a fall is a tragically common post-storm injury. Our team is trained and equipped to safely access and assess any roof, no matter the condition.

Step 2: Document Everything (Before You Touch Anything)

Your insurance claim starts now. Take extensive photos and videos of the damage from the safety of the ground. Capture images of shingles on the ground, dents in gutters, and any visible signs of wind damage to siding or windows. If water has entered your home, photograph water stains on ceilings and walls. This documentation is vital evidence for your insurance company.

Step 3: Make the First Call to Secure Your Property

Even a small breach in your roof can lead to catastrophic water damage inside your home. The most critical immediate action is emergency roof tarping. Our crews are staged and ready to deploy across the Gulf South to get your property protected, preventing a small problem from turning into a disaster.
