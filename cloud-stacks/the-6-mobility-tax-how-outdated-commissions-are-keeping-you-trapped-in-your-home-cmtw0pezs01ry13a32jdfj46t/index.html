A Brief History: Where Did the 6% Commission Come From?

The concept of a percentage-based commission became entrenched decades ago, long before information was freely available. Agents were the gatekeepers of all housing data, spending immense time and resources on marketing, manual paperwork, and connecting buyers and sellers. The 6% figure became a widely accepted "standard," though it has never been a law.

Recent industry-shaking lawsuits have put a national spotlight on this very issue, challenging the legality of how commissions are set and negotiated. This legal pressure is forcing the entire real estate world to re-evaluate this exact issue, confirming what we at 1 Percent Lists have been saying for years: the old way is broken. The future is about transparency, value, and putting the consumer first.
