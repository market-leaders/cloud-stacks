The Adjuster's Perspective vs. Your Home's Reality

Adjusters are trained professionals working to assess damage quickly and efficiently based on a specific set of criteria. Their job is to identify a "covered loss" and move on to the next claim in a storm-ravaged area. Your goal, however, is a complete, safe, and code-compliant roof restoration that protects your most significant investment for years to come. This often creates a gap between what is initially approved and what is actually required for a total repair.

Common Damage Items Missed in Louisiana & Mississippi Claims

An out-of-state adjuster, unfamiliar with the unique weather patterns from Baton Rouge to Biloxi, can easily overlook subtle but severe issues. We frequently see initial assessments miss critical items, such as:

  • Wind-lifted shingles that haven't creased but have broken seals, creating a pathway for water. These are common signs of wind damage that lead to slow leaks.
  • Damage to flashing, vents, and pipe boots, which are primary points of failure during high winds and driving rain.
  • Hidden water intrusion signs in the attic that indicate a compromised roof deck.
  • Code-required upgrades, like drip edge or ice & water shield, that are necessary for a proper replacement but may not be included in the initial scope of work.
