For homeowners in Covington, Mandeville, and Madisonville, 1 Percent List HUB provides all the same essential services for a much lower 1% listing fee, keeping thousands of dollars of your home's equity with you. ## The Anatomy of the Traditional 6% Commission The standard 6% real estate commission is not a single fee but is typically split four ways between two agents and their brokers. This is the most common source of confusion for sellers, who often believe their agent pockets the entire amount. In reality, the commission you pay is immediately divided to cover multiple parties and services that are essential to getting a home from listed to sold. Understanding this split is the first step to realizing how much of your equity is paying for legacy systems and overhead rather than direct service for your property. The entire commission structure was designed decades ago, long before the internet and modern technology made marketing and communication far more efficient. Let's break down where every percentage point goes. ### The Listing Side: Your Agent & Their Broker (~3%) When you agree to a 6% commission, roughly half of that, or 3%, is allocated to the brokerage representing you, the seller. However, your individual agent doesn't simply receive a check for 3% of your home's sale price. This amount is immediately split again with their managing broker (e.g., Keller Williams, RE/MAX, etc.). The broker's share is substantial and covers the significant overhead of running a real estate office. This includes costs like office rent, staff salaries, franchise fees paid to a national brand, professional liability (E&O) insurance, and access to proprietary software. These are fixed costs that the brokerage must cover regardless of how many homes are sold. The agent's remaining portion, which can be 50-80% of that 3% depending on their agreement with the broker, is what they use to run their personal business and market your home. This portion must cover: * **Direct Marketing Costs:** Professional photography, virtual tours, high-quality flyers, yard signs, and sophisticated lockboxes. * **MLS Fees:** Annual and per-listing fees to place your home on the Multiple Listing Service, the single most important tool for exposure. * **Digital Advertising:** Costs for premium placement on sites like [Realtor.com](https://www.realtor.com) and social media ad campaigns. * **Personal Expenses:** Gas for driving to showings, ongoing education requirements, licensing fees, and business insurance. * **Time & Expertise:** Their compensation for market analysis, pricing strategy, hosting open houses, coordinating showings, negotiating offers, and managing the complex paperwork from contract to closing. ### The Buyer's Side: The Buyer's Agent & Their Broker (~3%) The other half of the 6% commission is allocated to the buyer's side. This is a crucial, often misunderstood concept: as the seller, you traditionally pay the commission for the agent who brings the buyer to the table. While this may seem counterintuitive, it's the standard industry practice designed to incentivize every agent in the market to show your home to their pool of qualified buyers. Without this cooperative compensation, buyer's agents would have less financial motivation to show properties listed by other brokerages, drastically reducing your home's exposure. Just like on the listing side, this 3% is not a single payment to the buyer's agent. It is also paid to their brokerage first and then split. The buyer's agent's share compensates them for the extensive work they do, often over many months, including: * Identifying and qualifying potential buyers. * Searching the market and scheduling tours of homes, including beautiful [Northshore subdivisions](https://1percentlistshub.com/buy/northshore-subdivisions/). * Educating buyers on the local [Mandeville housing market trends](https://1percentlistshub.com/real-estate/mandeville-housing-market-trends/). * Drafting and submitting offers. * Negotiating on behalf of the buyer. * Coordinating inspections, appraisals, and the mortgage process. ## The Real Financial Impact on Covington & Mandeville Home Sellers For the average home sale in St. Tammany Parish, a 6% commission can cost sellers over $20,000 of their hard-earned equity.
