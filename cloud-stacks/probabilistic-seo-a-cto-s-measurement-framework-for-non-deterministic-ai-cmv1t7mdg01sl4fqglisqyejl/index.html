The goal was to reach position #1. It worked because search engines were essentially massive, indexed libraries that relied on a rigid set of signals, like backlinks and keyword density, to produce a largely uniform result page for most users. ### Q: Why is modern, AI-driven search "non-deterministic"? **A:** AI search is non-deterministic because the results are uniquely generated for each user's implicit and explicit context. An AI doesn't just "look up" an answer; it *constructs* one. It considers thousands of microdynamics: location, search history, time of day, and perceived intent, rendering a single "ranking" number utterly meaningless. ### Q: How does this specifically impact a local law firm, roofer, or real estate agent? **A:** For local and professional services, this shift is critical.
