The Hidden Cost of Selling Your Home in Atlanta

Traditional real estate commissions in Atlanta typically cost sellers 5-6% of their home's sale price, significantly impacting their net profit.

For most sellers, the commission fee is the single largest expense in the transaction. It's a percentage taken directly from the top of your sale price, before you see a dime of your proceeds. Understanding this structure is the first step toward reclaiming your hard-earned equity.

How the 6% Commission is Split in the Atlanta Metro Area

The standard commission isn't a single fee paid to one person. It's typically split down the middle between the two brokerages involved in the sale. The listing brokerage (representing you, the seller) receives half, and the buyer's brokerage receives the other half.

  • Listing Agent's Brokerage: Receives approximately 3% of the sale price for marketing your home, managing the process, and negotiating on your behalf.
  • Buyer's Agent's Brokerage: Receives approximately 3% of the sale price for bringing a qualified buyer to the table and representing their interests.

Crucially, the seller is responsible for paying both sides of this commission. This cost is deducted directly from your sale proceeds at the closing table, making it a direct hit to your net profit.
