The Problem: The Northshore's Insurance Roadblock

An older roof can make your Louisiana home nearly impossible for a buyer to insure, effectively killing the sale before it even begins. This issue has become a primary obstacle for sellers across the Northshore subdivisions and the greater New Orleans area.

The Northshore Insurance Gauntlet: Why Buyers are Walking Away

After a series of major storms, insurance companies have either fled Louisiana or drastically tightened their underwriting rules. This directly impacts your home sale. A buyer cannot get a mortgage without securing homeowner's insurance. If insurers refuse to write a policy because of the roof's age or condition, the buyer's financing falls through, and the deal is dead. This is a common story for homeowners from Covington to Mandeville.

The "15-Year Roof Rule": An Unwritten Law for Sellers

Insurers like Citizens Property Insurance and others now scrutinize roof age intensely. While not always an official, written policy, what has emerged is a powerful "unwritten rule" in the market: roofs over 15-20 years old are often an automatic "no" for new policies. As experts at Realtor.com note, an old roof is a major red flag for buyers and their lenders. Your roof might not be leaking, but its age alone is enough to stop a sale in its tracks.
