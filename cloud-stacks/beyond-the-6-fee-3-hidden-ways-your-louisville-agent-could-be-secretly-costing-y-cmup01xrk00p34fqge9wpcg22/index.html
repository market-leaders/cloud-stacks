Hidden Cost #1: An Agent's Pricing Strategy Can Cost You More Than Their Commission

Your agent's initial pricing strategy for your Louisville home can cost you more than their commission through stale listings or leaving money on the table. After you decide on an agent, setting the list price is the single most critical decision you'll make. Get it wrong, and you trigger a chain reaction that shrinks your final profit.

The Problem: The "Win the Listing" Price vs. The "Quick Sale" Price

Some agents will suggest an inflated price just to win your business, knowing full well you'll have to drop it later. By the time you do, your home is a "stale" listing. Buyers looking in competitive Louisville neighborhoods like The Highlands or St. Matthews start to wonder, "What's wrong with it?" This stigma invites lowball offers, costing you momentum and money. On the flip side, an agent focused on volume might suggest a price below market value for a quick, easy sale. Sure, it sells fast, but you could be leaving $10,000, $20,000, or more on the table—money that belongs in your pocket.
