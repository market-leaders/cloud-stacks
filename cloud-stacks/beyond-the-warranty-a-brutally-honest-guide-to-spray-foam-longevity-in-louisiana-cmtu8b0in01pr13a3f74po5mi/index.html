Open-Cell vs. Closed-Cell: The Longevity Champion for Our Humid Climate

While open-cell has its uses, closed-cell spray foam is the undisputed champion for longevity in Louisiana. It’s not just a recommendation; it’s a necessity for durability in our climate. Its dense structure provides critical advantages that open-cell simply cannot match.

The #1 Killer: Installation Errors and Trapped Moisture

This is the most critical point. The majority of spray foam failures we've remediated across Louisiana stem from these common, avoidable mistakes:

  • "Off-Ratio" Mix: When the two chemical components are mixed incorrectly, the foam fails to cure properly. This results in a spongy or brittle texture, lingering chemical smells, and significant shrinking away from studs, creating massive air gaps.
  • Improper Surface Prep: Applying foam to a damp or dirty surface in a humid New Orleans attic is a recipe for disaster. The foam won't adhere properly and can peel away over time, completely negating its air-sealing benefits.
  • Creating a "Moisture Sandwich": This is a huge problem in our climate. An inexperienced installer can trap moisture between the foam and the roof sheathing, leading to hidden rot that destroys your home's structure from the inside out.

Pest Pressure: A Brutally Honest Look at Termites and Foam

Let's clear this up: Can termites eat through spray foam? Yes, they can tunnel through almost anything. Do they eat it for food? No. Properly installed, dense closed-cell foam creates a solid, monolithic barrier that is difficult for pests to navigate. It eliminates the voids and pathways they love in fiberglass, making your home a less attractive target.

Hurricane-Force Winds and Foundation Shifting

In a hurricane, your home is under immense stress. Closed-cell spray foam acts like a structural glue, helping to hold your roof deck and walls together against high winds. This reinforcement can be the difference between minor damage and total roof failure. This is also why a stable foundation is key; the entire building envelope's integrity, from the foundation repair to the roofline, protects your insulation investment.
