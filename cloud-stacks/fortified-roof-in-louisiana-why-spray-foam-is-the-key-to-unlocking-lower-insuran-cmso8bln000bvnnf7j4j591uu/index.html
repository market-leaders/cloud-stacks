A FORTIFIED Roof isn't just a new set of shingles; it's a complete system engineered to a higher, rigorously tested standard. It's about building resilience from the deck up to defend against the specific threats we face in Louisiana. ### What is a FORTIFIED Roof? The Gold Standard in Resilience The core of the FORTIFIED standard is system-based thinking. It addresses the most common points of failure during a hurricane. Key requirements include: * **Stronger Edges:** Enhanced roof edges to prevent wind from getting underneath and starting the peeling process. * **Impact-Rated Shingles:** Shingles tested to resist damage from hail and wind-blown debris. * **Better Attachment:** Nails with a stronger bond and specific patterns to hold sheathing down during extreme winds. * **Sealed Roof Deck:** A crucial secondary water barrier applied to the roof deck to keep water out if the primary covering is lost. ### Why New Orleans Insurers Reward FORTIFIED Homes The reason for the savings is simple: risk reduction. Insurers see a FORTIFIED-certified home as a much safer bet, especially in a high-wind zone like coastal Louisiana. According to the Insurance Information Institute, strengthening homes against disasters is a key strategy for managing insurance availability and affordability. This reduced risk translates directly into significant wind mitigation credits, which can slash your annual premiums. ## The Key Component: Why Spray Foam is the Superior Choice for a Sealed Roof Deck ### Closed-cell spray foam insulation is the superior method for creating the "sealed roof deck" required by the FORTIFIED Roof standard, acting as a powerful adhesive that bonds the roof deck to the framing. While other methods exist to seal a roof deck, none offer the comprehensive structural and performance benefits of closed-cell spray foam. It transforms a standard roof into a monolithic, reinforced structure. ### The Sealed Roof Deck: Your Home's Secondary Water Barrier Imagine a hurricane rips shingles from your roof. With a standard roof, water immediately pours into your attic, destroying insulation, ceilings, and everything below. The sealed roof deck is a waterproof layer applied directly to the wood sheathing that prevents this catastrophic water intrusion, giving you a critical line of defense. ### Spray Foam vs. Traditional Methods (Tape & Membranes) When it comes to creating this sealed deck, the choice of material is critical.
