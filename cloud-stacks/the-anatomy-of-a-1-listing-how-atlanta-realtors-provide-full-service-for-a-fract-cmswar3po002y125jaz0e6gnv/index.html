The Old Model - Deconstructing the 6% Commission in Atlanta

Atlanta homeowners traditionally pay 5-6% in total real estate commissions, a cost that significantly reduces their net proceeds from a sale. This long-standing commission structure has been the norm for decades, but many Atlanta sellers question if it still reflects fair value in today's digital world, where AI and proptech are changing the game.

How is the 6% Commission Typically Split in an Atlanta Home Sale?

The total commission is paid entirely by the seller at closing. It is then typically split down the middle between the brokerage representing the seller (the listing side) and the brokerage representing the buyer (the buyer's side).

| Party | Commission Percentage | | :--- | :--- | | Listing Agent's Brokerage | 3% | | Buyer's Agent's Brokerage | 3% | | **Total Commission Paid by Seller** | **6%** |
