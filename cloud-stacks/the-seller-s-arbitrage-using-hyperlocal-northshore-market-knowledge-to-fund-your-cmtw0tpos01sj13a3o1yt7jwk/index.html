The Traditional 6% Commission: A Northshore Standard That's No Longer Necessary

The standard real estate commission model has long been the norm, but technology and efficient business models have rendered it obsolete. Typically, a 6% total commission is split between the two agents involved in the transaction: 3% goes to the listing agent (your agent) and 3% goes to the buyer's agent. While you still need to compensate the agent who brings the buyer, the 3% you pay your own agent is no longer a fixed price.

Consider a realistic Northshore home price. If you sell your Mandeville home for $450,000, a traditional 6% commission amounts to a staggering $27,000 paid from your equity. Of that, $13,500 goes to your listing agent. By accepting this as standard, you are effectively leaving a massive amount of your own money on the table—money that could have stayed in your pocket.
