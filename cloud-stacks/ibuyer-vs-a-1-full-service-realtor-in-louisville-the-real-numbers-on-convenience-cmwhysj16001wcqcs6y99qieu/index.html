What is an iBuyer and how do they work in Louisville?

An iBuyer is a large real estate company that uses automated valuation technology to make a quick, all-cash offer on your Louisville home. These companies aim to buy properties slightly under market value, perform minor updates, and resell them for a profit, acting more like a high-tech house flipper than a traditional buyer.

The Promise: A Fast, Hassle-Free Sale

The primary benefit of an iBuyer is speed. You submit your home's details online and can receive a preliminary offer within 24-48 hours. If you accept, you can close in as little as two weeks, bypassing the need to maintain a show-ready home, deal with public showings, or face the uncertainty of waiting for a qualified buyer.

The Reality: The Hidden Costs of Convenience

This speed comes with significant costs. iBuyer offers are typically below what your home could fetch on the open market. Furthermore, they charge a "service fee" that often ranges from 5% to 10% of the sale price. After their in-person inspection, they will also likely ask for a credit to cover the cost of any needed repairs, further reducing your final payout.
