What Does a 6% Commission Actually Pay For?

A traditional 6% commission is typically split down the middle. 3% goes to the brokerage representing the seller (the listing brokerage), and 3% goes to the brokerage representing the buyer (the buyer's brokerage). From that 3% listing fee, your agent must then pay a portion to their own brokerage, covering franchise fees, office space, and administrative support. This multi-layered split is why the percentage has remained stubbornly high for so long.

Our model at 1 Percent Lists Mile High is different. We charge just 1% on the listing side while still offering a competitive commission to the buyer's agent (typically 2-3%, at your discretion). By streamlining our operations and eliminating the overhead of traditional firms, we can offer the best broker to list your Denver home with full, expert service while leaving significantly more of your equity in your pocket.
