What is a buyer buydown and how can it help sell my home?

A buyer buydown is a seller-funded concession that temporarily lowers the buyer's mortgage interest rate, making their initial monthly payments more affordable. This powerful incentive directly addresses buyer concerns about high rates, making your home significantly more attractive than competing properties in sought-after communities like Beau Chene.

How a 2-1 Buydown Works: A Simple Example

The most common type of buydown is the 2-1 buydown. It's a straightforward concept that provides significant relief to buyers during their first two years of homeownership. The seller pays a one-time fee at closing, which is held in an escrow account by the lender and used to subsidize the buyer's monthly payments. Here’s the breakdown:
  • Year 1: The buyer's interest rate is 2% lower than the locked rate.
  • Year 2: The buyer's interest rate is 1% lower than the locked rate.
  • Years 3-30: The rate returns to the original locked rate for the remainder of the loan.
The cost to the seller is the sum of the interest savings over those first two years. This is often far less than a major price reduction but has a much greater psychological and financial impact on the buyer.
