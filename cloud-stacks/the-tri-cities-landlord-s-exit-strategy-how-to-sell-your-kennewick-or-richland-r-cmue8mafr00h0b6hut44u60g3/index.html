Part 1: Your Pre-Listing Game Plan: Critical Decisions for Tri-Cities Landlords

Selling a rental property for top dollar in Kennewick or Richland begins long before a 'For Sale' sign ever touches the yard. Your initial strategic choices regarding tenants, timing, and property condition will have the single biggest impact on your final net profit.

The Tenant Dilemma: Selling Occupied vs. Selling Vacant

Deciding whether to list your property with tenants in place or wait until it's vacant is your first major hurdle. Each approach targets a different buyer pool and comes with a unique set of financial trade-offs.

| Strategy | Pros | Cons | | :--- | :--- | :--- | | **Selling with Tenants** | Continue receiving rental income. Attracts other investors looking for a turnkey property. | Showings are difficult to coordinate. The property may not show well. Tenants can be uncooperative, limiting your buyer pool. | | **Selling Vacant** | Easy to show. You can make repairs and stage the home to appeal to the widest audience (homeowners, not just investors), often leading to a higher sale price. | No rental income during the listing period. You are responsible for all carrying costs (utilities, mortgage). |

The Kenmore Team's listing specialists provide a data-backed recommendation for your specific property in Richland or Kennewick, analyzing current market absorption rates for both tenant-occupied and vacant homes to guide your decision.
