Demystifying the Fideicomiso: How Americans Own Coastal Property

The most common question we get from American buyers is, "Can I actually own property in Mexico?" The answer is a resounding yes, through a mechanism called a fideicomiso. This is a bank trust, established by the Mexican government specifically for foreign buyers to purchase property in coastal and border regions. It's not a lease; it's a secure and renewable trust that grants you, the beneficiary, all the rights of ownership. You can use, enjoy, rent, sell, and will the property to your heirs, just as you would in the U.S. It's a standard, safe, and time-tested process.
