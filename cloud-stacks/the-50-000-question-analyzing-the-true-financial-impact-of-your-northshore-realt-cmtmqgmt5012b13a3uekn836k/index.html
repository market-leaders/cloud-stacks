How the Standard 6% Commission is Split (and Why You Pay It All)

The 6% is typically divided equally between two parties. Three percent goes to the agent who lists your home (the seller's agent), and the other three percent goes to the agent who brings the buyer (the buyer's agent). As the seller, this entire 6% comes directly out of your sale proceeds before you see a dime. It's your equity paying for both sides of the transaction.
