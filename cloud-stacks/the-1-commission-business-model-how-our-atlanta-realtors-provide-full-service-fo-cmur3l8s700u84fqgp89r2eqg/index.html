Understanding the Standard 6% Commission Split in Georgia

The typical 6% commission isn't a single fee paid to one person. It's usually split evenly between two parties: the listing agent (who represents you, the seller) and the buyer's agent (who brings the buyer to the table). This means 3% goes to your agent's brokerage and 3% goes to the buyer's agent's brokerage. Crucially, as the seller, you are responsible for paying both sides of this commission from your sale proceeds.
