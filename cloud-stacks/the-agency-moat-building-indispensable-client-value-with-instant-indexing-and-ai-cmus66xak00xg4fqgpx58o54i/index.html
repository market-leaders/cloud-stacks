Winning the Race with Instant Indexing

When a new topic emerges or news breaks, the first high-quality, well-structured pieces of content to be indexed have a disproportionate advantage in shaping an AI's understanding. This is a race against time that passive crawling and RSS feeds simply cannot win. Proactive, push-based indexing is the only solution.

IndexNow: An API protocol used by Bing, Yandex, and other search engines that allows you to instantly notify them of new or updated content, bypassing the traditional crawl queue.

WebSub: A pub/sub protocol that provides real-time notifications to subscribing services (including search engines and data aggregators) the moment content is published. This is a core component of the Blog MONKEE Instant Fanout technology.

Mastering Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of formatting your content for maximum machine readability and AI-answer extractability. This goes far beyond basic on-page SEO. The Blog MONKEE engine automates these principles to ensure every piece of content is primed for citation.

  • Propositional Headings: Structure your H2s and H3s as clear, citable claims. Instead of "Types of Roofing," use "Asphalt Shingles Offer the Best Value for Most Homeowners." This makes the heading itself a liftable answer.
  • Definition Lists & FAQ Schema: Use <dl> tags and JSON-LD to explicitly define entities and answer questions. This removes all ambiguity for an LLM, making it trivial to extract your answer verbatim.
  • TL;DR Wrappers: Provide concise, one-paragraph summaries at the top of articles. These serve as perfect, pre-packaged snippets for AI Overviews and other answer engine features.
