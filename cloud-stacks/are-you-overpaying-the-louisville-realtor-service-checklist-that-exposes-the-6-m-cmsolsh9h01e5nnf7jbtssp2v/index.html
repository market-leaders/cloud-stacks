This knowledge empowers you to question the status quo and explore modern, more efficient brokerage models that prioritize your financial outcome. ### Deconstructing the 6% "Standard" in the Louisville Real Estate Market For years, the 6% commission became an industry tradition simply through repetition. It was what agents quoted and what sellers came to expect. However, it's crucial to understand that commission rates are, and have always been, negotiable. In fact, federal antitrust laws strictly prohibit real estate companies from colluding to set or "fix" commission rates. This means there is no such thing as a "set" or "standard" rate across the industry. Recent events, including the major NAR commission settlement, have brought this issue to the forefront, further clarifying that sellers have the power to negotiate the fees they pay. The idea that you *must* pay a certain percentage to sell your home is simply not true. Smart Louisville sellers are now realizing they have options, and they are choosing the one that offers the best value. ### How a Typical Commission is Split in a Louisville Home Sale To see why the 6% figure is so high, it helps to understand where the money goes. Traditionally, that 6% is split right down the middle between the two brokerages involved in the transaction. * **3% for the Listing Agent's Brokerage:** This is the fee paid to the agent and brokerage representing you, the seller. * **3% for the Buyer's Agent's Brokerage:** This is the fee offered to the agent and brokerage who bring a qualified buyer to the table. Each of these agents then splits their portion with their respective brokerage firm. While this structure is straightforward, it was created in a pre-internet era. Today, technology and more efficient business models allow for the same, if not better, service at a much lower cost to the seller. ### The Real Cost to Your Louisville Home Equity: A Quick Calculation Percentages can feel abstract, but the dollar amount is very real.
