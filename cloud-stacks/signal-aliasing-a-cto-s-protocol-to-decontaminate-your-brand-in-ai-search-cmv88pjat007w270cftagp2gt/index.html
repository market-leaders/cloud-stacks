TL;DR

What is signal aliasing and how do you fix it?
Signal aliasing is when AI search engines get confused by conflicting data about your business online, causing them to provide inaccurate information or recommend competitors. You fix it by executing a decontamination protocol: 1) Audit all brand mentions to find inconsistencies. 2) Consolidate your Name, Address, Phone, and URL (NAPU) into a single source of truth on your website. 3) Deploy structured data (Schema markup) to explicitly define your services, location, and identity for machines. This process purifies your digital signal, making your business the authoritative, trustworthy choice for AI-powered search.

What is signal aliasing in plain English?

Question: My business has been at the same address for 10 years. Why would an AI get it wrong?

Answer:
Because the internet never forgets. Signal aliasing is digital chaos. It’s the ghost of your old office address in a third-party directory. It’s the service you stopped offering in 2018 still listed on a review site. It’s another plumbing company with a similar name in the next town over.

Each of these data points is a "signal." When they conflict, the signal-to-noise ratio collapses. AI, which relies on statistical certainty, cannot determine the ground truth. Instead of defaulting to your website, it may average the data, ignore you entirely, or worse, confidently present the wrong information to a potential client. Your brand's authority is diluted by the microdynamics of bad data across the web.
