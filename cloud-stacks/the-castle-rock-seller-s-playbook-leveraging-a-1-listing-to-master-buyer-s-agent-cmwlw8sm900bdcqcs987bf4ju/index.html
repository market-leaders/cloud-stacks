What is the seller's role in setting the buyer's agent commission?

As the seller, you decide the compensation offered to the buyer's agent, which is a crucial marketing tool for your Castle Rock property. This commission, often called the "co-op" or "Buyer's Agent Commission" (BAC), is not fixed. It is an amount you choose to offer to incentivize agents to show and sell your home, and it directly influences how agents working with active buyers perceive your listing.

Why a Competitive Co-op Commission Matters

The BAC you offer is advertised on the Multiple Listing Service (MLS) to all local real estate agents. A competitive offer ensures your home gets maximum exposure from agents representing qualified buyers in the Castle Rock area. An uncompetitive offer could result in fewer agents prioritizing your home for showings, potentially limiting your pool of buyers.
