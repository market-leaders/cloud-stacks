The Hidden Costs of a "Bookings-Only" Business Model

When your entire business model depends on a third-party platform, you're not the one in control. Your revenue is capped by what the algorithm decides your property is worth on any given night, minus hefty commissions. You're constantly fighting for visibility against a sea of other listings, and you have no direct, lasting relationship with your own guests. This model frames your property as a commodity, not the unique asset it is, leaving it under-monetized and vulnerable.

The Guest Experience "Upgrade" That Pays for Itself

Ancillary offers aren't just about making more money; they create a more memorable, convenient, and personalized stay. Imagine a guest arriving late after a long flight. Instead of having to find an open store in an unfamiliar neighborhood, they are thrilled to find they can purchase a local craft beer and some snacks directly from your in-home pantry. This small convenience transforms a moment of stress into one of delight, directly impacting their review and their likelihood of booking with you again.

The In-Home Shop: The Honor Pantry

Honor Pantry: A curated selection of snacks, drinks, local goods, and travel essentials available for guests to purchase directly in the unit. Forget the clunky cash box of the past. A modern Honor Pantry uses a simple, QR-code-driven system for frictionless payment. Guests scan, select their items on their phone, and pay in seconds. The ASTRO Honor Pantry program provides the framework and tech to set this up in minutes, turning your kitchen counter into a small-scale retail space.

The Local Expert: The Digital Concierge

Digital Concierge: A service that offers pre-vetted, bookable local experiences, earning you a commission on every booking. You become the trusted local guide without having to make a single phone call. Through a digital portal, guests can browse and book services from your trusted local partners—from a private chef for a special dinner to an in-home massage, guided tours, or baby gear rentals. The ASTRO Concierge program connects you with these partners and provides a ready-made digital interface via the Guest Portal.

The Showroom: The Home Showcase & AArtners

Home Showcase: A program that makes the items in your home shoppable. Guests constantly ask where you got your decor, that comfortable mattress, or the unique coffee mugs. Now, you can let them buy it. A simple QR code placed next to an item can link to a page where a guest can purchase it directly from the maker or a retailer, with you earning a commission. The ASTRO Home Showcase and AArtners programs facilitate this, turning your entire property into a live-in catalog and a platform for local artists and artisans.

Localizing your "storefront" offerings is crucial for success, and a strong owner network makes it achievable.

The most successful ancillary revenue strategies are deeply rooted in the local character of your property's location, which requires both market intelligence and a network of reliable partners. Generic offerings fall flat; guests crave authenticity that connects them to the place they're visiting. This is where community and local expertise become your most valuable assets.

Tapping into Your Local Scene: What to Offer

The best ancillary offerings feel authentic and specific. A one-size-fits-all approach doesn't work. You need to think about what makes your city unique and how you can package that experience for your guests.

  • Mountain Town (e.g., Aspen, CO): Stock your Honor Pantry with high-quality trail mix, electrolyte drinks, and local microbrews. Use the Concierge to offer ski and snowboard rental delivery or guided fly-fishing trips. Showcase stunning mountain photography from a local artist via AArtners.
  • Beach City (e.g., Miami, FL): Fill the fridge with local kombucha, cold-pressed juices, and stock the bathroom with high-quality aloe vera gel. Offer private boat charters, jet ski rentals, or a beach chair and umbrella setup service. Feature vibrant, coastal-inspired art from the local scene.
  • Music City (e.g., Gatlinburg, TN): Your Honor Pantry could feature locally distilled whiskey, Goo Goo Clusters, and artisanal hot chicken spice rubs. Your Concierge could offer pre-booked tickets to the Grand Ole Opry or private songwriting sessions.
