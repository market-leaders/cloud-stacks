This total commission is usually split down the middle between two different brokerages. Let's break it down: * **Listing Agent's Brokerage:** Receives 2.5-3% for representing the seller. This fee covers marketing the home, advising the seller, and managing the transaction. * **Buyer's Agent's Brokerage:** Receives 2.5-3% for bringing the buyer to the table. The seller pays this fee as well, even though this agent represents the buyer's best interests. This system was established long before the internet, Zillow, and digital marketing revolutionized how we find and sell homes. In that pre-internet era, agents did heavy lifting finding buyers through print ads and exclusive office listings.
