Part 1: Your Equity Is At Risk If Your Agent Can't Justify Their Commission

The most critical questions you can ask revolve around the math. A modern agent should be able to clearly articulate their value and demonstrate how their fee structure is designed to maximize your net profit, not just their paycheck. These questions pull back the curtain on the traditional 6% model.

Question 1: "Can you provide a detailed net sheet breaking down your full commission structure and showing my estimated proceeds at several potential sale prices?"

  • Why it matters: This forces transparency from the start. It moves the conversation from a vague percentage to a concrete dollar amount you'll pay, showing the real cost to sell a house.
  • Red Flag Answer: They are hesitant, focus only on the gross sale price, or use confusing jargon to obscure the total cost.
  • The Modern Answer (The 1% Model): "Absolutely. Here’s a net sheet showing our 1% listing fee next to a traditional 3% fee. On a $400,000 home in Hamilton County, that’s a direct savings of $8,000 for you, without sacrificing any of the services you need."

Question 2: "Indianapolis home values have soared. How has your service evolved to justify a commission that has increased proportionally with home prices?"

  • Why it matters: This addresses the "unearned raise" traditional agents have received due to market appreciation. Technology has made their job more efficient, yet their percentage-based pay has skyrocketed.
  • Red Flag Answer: "That's just how the industry works," or "A higher price means more work and liability for me."
  • The Modern Answer: "Our model recognizes that technology should create efficiency and savings. We pass those savings directly to you with a fair, flat 1% listing fee, regardless of market appreciation. Your equity is yours to keep."

Question 3: "What specific, high-impact services are covered by the listing side of your commission?"

  • Why it matters: It forces them to itemize their value. When a 3% listing agent lists the same services as a 1% listing agent (MLS listing, photos, negotiation), the value proposition of the high fee begins to crumble.
  • Red Flag Answer: A generic list of services without connecting them to the high cost.
  • The Modern Answer: "For our 1% listing fee, you receive the complete full-service package: professional photography, a 3D tour, premium MLS placement, expert negotiation, and full contract-to-close management. We've just eliminated the bloated costs."

Question 4: "Is your commission rate negotiable?"

  • Why it matters: This question tests their business model's flexibility. Rigid, non-negotiable fees often indicate a brokerage focused on protecting its own profits over the client's.
  • Red Flag Answer: A flat "no" or the justification that their brokerage "doesn't allow it."
  • The Modern Answer: "Our model is built on a transparent, low fee from the start, so there's no need for uncomfortable haggling. We offer our best price—1%—to every single client because it's fair and efficient."

Question 5: "Beyond just selling my house, how does your business model actively help me protect my equity?"

  • Why it matters: This frames the relationship correctly. A great agent is an equity protection partner.
  • Red Flag Answer: They talk only about getting the highest sale price, ignoring the massive cost of their own commission.
  • The Modern Answer: "Equity protection is our entire mission. We do it in two ways: first, by using a data-driven marketing strategy to secure top dollar for your home, and second, by charging a 1% listing fee that keeps an average of $10,000-$15,000 of your equity in your pocket."

Part 2: An Outdated Marketing Plan in Indianapolis Means Leaving Money on the Table

In today's market, selling a home in Carmel, Fishers, or Broad Ripple is a digital game. If an agent is still talking about park benches and newspaper ads, they are wasting your money on their own brand awareness instead of investing in targeted strategies that find qualified buyers for your home.

Question 6: "What is your digital marketing strategy for a home like mine in this specific Indiana neighborhood?"

  • Why it matters: This demands a hyper-local, tech-forward answer. A generic plan won't cut it.
  • Red Flag Answer: Vague talk about "the MLS" and "syndication to websites." They may mention an open house as their primary strategy.
  • The Modern Answer: "We'll launch a targeted social media ad campaign aimed at buyers with demographics matching this area, create a high-conversion online listing with a 3D tour, and use SEO to ensure your home appears at the top of searches for 'your area homes for sale'."

Question 7: "How do you leverage technology and data to price my home and target buyers?"

  • Why it matters: It separates agents who use data from those who just "go with their gut."
  • Red Flag Answer: "I have 20 years of experience, I know this market."
  • The Modern Answer: "We use a combination of real-time MLS data, local market absorption rates, and digital buyer activity trends to price your home competitively. Our marketing tech then targets buyers based on their online search behavior, putting your home in front of the most likely purchasers."

Question 8: "What percentage of your marketing budget is spent on digital vs. print?"

  • Why it matters: This is a direct question about efficiency. Print marketing is expensive and has a low ROI for a specific property; it's about branding the agent. Digital is targeted and measurable.
  • Red Flag Answer: They are proud of their magazine spreads or direct mail campaigns.
  • The Modern Answer: "Nearly 100% is digital. We've cut the wasteful overhead of print and expensive office suites, and we pass that operational efficiency on to you as savings."

Question 9: "How will you measure and report on your marketing efforts for my home?"

  • Why it matters: It demands accountability. You should know how many people are seeing your listing and where they are coming from.
  • Red Flag Answer: "The proof is in the showings."
  • The Modern Answer: "You'll receive regular updates with key metrics, including online views, social media engagement, and the number of virtual tours taken. We believe in total transparency."

Question 10: "How does your brokerage's overhead, like a large physical office, impact the fees you charge?"

  • Why it matters: This directly links their business costs to your commission.
  • Red Flag Answer: They defend their office as a sign of success or a place to meet clients.
  • The Modern Answer: "We've eliminated the need for expensive brick-and-mortar offices. Our agents are mobile and tech-equipped, which dramatically reduces our overhead. That efficiency is the core reason we can offer a full-service experience for a 1% listing fee."

Part 3: 'Full-Price' Commission Doesn't Automatically Guarantee Full-Service Results

The biggest fear homeowners have about a lower commission is a reduction in service. These questions are designed to get a detailed commitment from an agent, allowing you to compare their "full-price" promises directly against the full-service guarantee of the 1% model.

Question 11: "Can you provide a checklist of every service included from listing to closing?"

  • Why it matters: This gets their promises in writing. You can use this checklist to compare agents and hold them accountable.
  • Red Flag Answer: A verbal, high-level overview that lacks specific details.
  • The Modern Answer: "Of course. Here is our 25-point full-service checklist. It includes everything from professional photography and yard signs to managing inspection negotiations and coordinating with the title company. You sacrifice nothing."

Question 12: "Will I be working directly with you through the entire process?"

  • Why it matters: Many top-producing traditional agents pass clients off to a junior team member after the contract is signed.
  • Red Flag Answer: "I oversee my team, but my transaction coordinator will handle the details."
  • The Modern Answer: "Yes. You will have my direct cell phone number. I will be your dedicated point of contact from our first meeting to the closing table."

Question 13: "What commission do you recommend offering the buyer's agent, and why is that important?"

  • Why it matters: This uncovers their understanding of market dynamics and exposes the "secret sauce" of the 1% model.
  • Red Flag Answer: They are vague or suggest offering a low/non-competitive rate to save money.
  • The Modern Answer: "We recommend offering a competitive buyer's agent commission, typically 2.5-3%. This ensures all agents in the Indianapolis area are motivated to show your home, maximizing your exposure and driving a faster, higher-priced sale. Our savings are on the listing side; we never compromise on buyer incentives."

Question 14: "What is your strategy for handling multiple offers and inspection negotiations?"

  • Why it matters: This is where an agent's skill truly shines. Their answer reveals their experience and commitment to your financial outcome.
  • Red Flag Answer: A simple, "I'll present all offers to you."
  • The Modern Answer: "We'll create a spreadsheet to compare offers beyond just price, looking at terms, financing, and contingencies. For inspections, we have a network of trusted local contractors to quickly assess any requests, ensuring we negotiate from a position of strength to protect your bottom line."

Question 15: "Can you share case studies of homes you've recently sold in the Indianapolis area?"

  • Why it matters: This is the ultimate proof. It demonstrates their track record of success right here in your local market.
  • Red Flag Answer: They talk about their brokerage's overall sales, not their own personal, recent transactions.
  • The Modern Answer: "Absolutely. Here are three recent sales in Zionsville and Carmel. You can see the professional marketing we used, the sale price we achieved, and a testimonial from the homeowner who saved over $12,000 in commissions."
