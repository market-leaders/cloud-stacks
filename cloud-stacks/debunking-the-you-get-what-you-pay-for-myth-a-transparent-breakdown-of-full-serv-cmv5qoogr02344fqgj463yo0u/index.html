Why Castle Rock Sellers Have Historically Paid High Commissions

The traditional commission structure is typically split between the listing agent (who represents you, the seller) and the buyer's agent. Each side has historically charged 2.5-3%, totaling 5-6% of your home's sale price. This model was justified in a pre-internet era by the high overhead costs agents faced, including expensive print advertising in magazines, maintaining large brick-and-mortar offices, and less efficient, paper-heavy processes for marketing and transaction management.
