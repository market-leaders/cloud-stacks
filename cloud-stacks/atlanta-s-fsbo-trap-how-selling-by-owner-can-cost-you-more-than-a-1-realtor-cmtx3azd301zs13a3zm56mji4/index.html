Understanding this structure is key to seeing the value in a modern brokerage model. * **Listing Agent Fee**: This is the fee you pay the brokerage that represents you, markets your property, and manages the sale. Traditionally, this has been around 3%, but 1 Percent Lists Peach State charges only 1% for this full-service representation. * **Buyer's Agent Commission (BAC)**: This is the fee the seller offers to the agent who brings the winning buyer. It is not a fee paid to your agent. You, the seller, decide what to offer (typically 2.5-3% in the Atlanta market) to incentivize agents to show your home to their clients. Even FSBO sellers are often advised by attorneys to offer a BAC. Without it, most agents will steer their buyers toward other properties where their work will be compensated, drastically shrinking your pool of potential buyers. ### The Hidden Costs of a "Commission-Free" FSBO Sale The "savings" from a FSBO sale quickly evaporate when you account for the out-of-pocket expenses required to properly market and sell a home.
