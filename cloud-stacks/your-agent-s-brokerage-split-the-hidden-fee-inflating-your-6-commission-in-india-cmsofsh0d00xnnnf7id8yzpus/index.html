The brokerage split is the percentage of the commission that a real estate agent pays to their managing brokerage on every single transaction. In short, you're paying for their brand, not just your agent's service. * **The Numbers:** This split can range from 30% to 50%. For a newer agent, it's often a 50/50 split.
