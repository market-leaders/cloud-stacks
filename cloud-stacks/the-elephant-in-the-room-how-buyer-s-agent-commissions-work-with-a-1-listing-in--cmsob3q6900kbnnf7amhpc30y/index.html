The "Secret Sauce": How Our Hybrid Commission Model Actually Works

First, let's break down the two parts of a typical real estate commission: the Listing Agent Commission and the Buyer's Agent Commission (BAC). In a traditional 6% model, this is often split right down the middle: 3% to the agent listing the home and 3% to the agent who brings the buyer. This structure has remained largely unchanged for decades.

Our modern model provides unbundled transparency. The formula is simple: 1% Listing Fee (to us) + Competitive BAC (you choose, we advise 2-2.5%) = Your Total Commission. This structure makes your home just as attractive to buyer's agents as any other property on the MIBOR BLC (the local MLS). You don't miss out on potential buyers coming from F.C. Tucker, Compass, or any other brokerage in town.
