It’s a system that has benefited traditional brokerages for generations, but it’s time to pull back the curtain and see where that money really goes. ### What is the 6% Commission and Where Does It Really Go? The 6% is typically split down the middle. 3% goes to the brokerage representing the seller (the listing agent's side), and 3% goes to the brokerage representing the buyer (the buyer's agent's side). From there, each brokerage takes a cut before paying their respective agents. The critical point is that you, the seller, are funding the entire transaction. This commission structure was cemented long before the internet, Zillow, or digital contracts. Yet, as technology streamlined every aspect of an agent's job, the price for consumers never adjusted. This is the core problem: the industry became more efficient, but the savings were never passed on to you. ### The Real-Dollar Impact on Your Nest Egg Let's make this tangible.
