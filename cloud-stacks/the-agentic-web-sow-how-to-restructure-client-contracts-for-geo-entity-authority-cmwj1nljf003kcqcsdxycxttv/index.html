This means updating your SOW with new deliverables that prove your client's visibility within AI answer engines, a complex process that can be automated at scale with a platform like [Blog MONKEE](https://blog.monkee.ai/). ## Why Are Traditional SEO Contracts Suddenly Obsolete? Traditional SEO contracts are obsolete because their core metrics, like clicks and SERP positions, are being systematically eroded by AI answer engines that intercept user traffic before it ever reaches a client's website. This section explains why the old model of value is broken and why agencies must adapt their client agreements now to avoid inevitable churn. ### The "Zero-Click" Threat is Now the "AI-Answer" Reality For years, we discussed the "zero-click search" as a looming threat. That threat is now the dominant reality. AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT don't just point users to an answer; they synthesize it directly on the results page, often eliminating any need to click on a traditional blue link. This fundamentally breaks the correlation between ranking and traffic. Consider this scenario: your client, a roofer in Denver, ranks #1 for "best roofer in Denver." A decade ago, this was a guaranteed win. Today, Google's AI Overview might summarize the top three roofers, pull in reviews, and provide a direct answer, satisfying the user's intent completely. Your client holds the top spot but sees their lead flow and traffic decline, creating a massive disconnect between your reporting and their business results. ### Your Old KPIs (GSC Clicks, Impressions) Are Misleading The reporting tools we've relied on are no longer fit for purpose. Google Search Console doesn't accurately report when your content is used as a source to generate an AI answer without a click-through. Your client's content could be the foundational source for a dozen AI Overviews, establishing them as an authority in the knowledge graph, but your GSC dashboard will show flat or declining impressions and clicks. This creates a dangerous value-delivery gap. You are doing the essential work of informing the AI and building your client's entity, but your reporting fails to capture this value. Without new metrics, you have no way to justify your retainer when the old numbers no longer tell the whole story. The conversation about [citation velocity](https://blog.monkee.ai/citation-velocity-new-agency-kpi-agentic-web/) is becoming more important than click velocity. ### The Client Conversation You Need to Avoid This reporting gap leads to an inevitable and painful conversation. Imagine your client on a quarterly review call: "My GSC traffic is flat, my leads are down, and I just saw my main competitor cited by name in three different AI answers for our top keywords. What exactly am I paying you for?" This isn't a hypothetical; it's the new reality for agencies clinging to old SOWs. It highlights the direct business risk of not adapting. The agencies that survive will be the ones who get ahead of this conversation by restructuring their contracts around the metrics of the agentic web. ## What New Deliverables Should Agencies Include in an Agentic Web SOW? Agencies should include deliverables like Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) scores, geo-targeted entity mentions, and multi-engine visibility reports in an agentic web SOW. This provides a tangible, new contract structure that proves value in a post-Google world and aligns your work with what actually drives authority and visibility today. ### Deliverable 1: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) This is the foundational deliverable for the new SOW. It moves beyond on-page SEO and focuses on making content perfectly consumable for large language models. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**: The practice of structuring, formatting, and marking up content specifically for optimal extraction, interpretation, and citation by AI models and answer engines. **What to Sell:** Instead of just "blog posts," you'll sell content engineered for AI. Your SOW should include monthly reporting on the volume of content produced and optimized with AI-first principles. This includes the implementation of definition lists, TL;DR summaries, highly structured H2s/H3s, and the technical schema (like FAQ and JSON-LD) that directly signals answers to AI crawlers. This is about proving you understand [content provenance](https://blog.monkee.ai/content-provenance-win-age-ai-indexing-apis/) in the age of AI. ### Deliverable 2: Geo-Targeted Entity Authority For local businesses, this is the most critical new KPI. It shifts the focus from ranking for a keyword to becoming the recognized entity for a service in a specific location. **Geo-Targeted Entity Authority**: A measurement of how frequently a client's brand name is mentioned as an authoritative entity for a specific service within a defined geographic area across AI chat responses, summaries, and overviews. **What to Sell:** You're no longer just building links; you're running "Mention Campaigns." Your SOW should outline a report that tracks the growth of the client's brand association with key commercial terms. This means demonstrating, with screenshots and logs, that when a user asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI for "[Service]," your client's name appears with increasing frequency and prominence. This is a direct measure of their authority in the knowledge graph. ### Deliverable 3: Multi-Engine Visibility & Indexing Speed The agentic web is decentralized. Relying solely on Google is a critical error. True authority means being everywhere, instantly, ensuring your client's information is part of the training data for all current and future models. **Multi-Engine Visibility**: A report confirming that content has been published, syndicated, and indexed across a diverse ecosystem of search engines (Google, Bing, Yandex) and data hubs, not just a single platform. **What to Sell:** Your SOW should include a "Syndication & Indexing Velocity" report. This deliverable proves that every piece of content is pushed immediately to indexing APIs like IndexNow (which powers Bing and Yandex) and WebSub hubs. This demonstrates broad and rapid information distribution, proving your work is feeding the entire web, not just one corner of it. This is how you build a defensible moat for your clients that transcends any single algorithm update. ## How Can Agencies Profitably Deliver GEO and Entity Authority at Scale?
