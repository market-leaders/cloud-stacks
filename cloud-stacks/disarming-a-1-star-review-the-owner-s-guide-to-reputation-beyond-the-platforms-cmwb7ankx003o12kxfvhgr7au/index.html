What is the first step to take after getting a 1-star review?

The first step is to pause, analyze the feedback objectively for any truth, and craft a professional public response within the platform's guidelines. Your goal is to show future guests that you are a responsive and responsible host, not to win an argument with the previous guest.

Deconstructing the Complaint

Before you type a single word, take a breath and read the review with an open mind. Is the feedback valid? Maybe the Wi-Fi was actually down or the cleaning was missed. Is it a matter of mismatched expectations, like a guest who thought your quiet cabin in the woods would be "closer to downtown"? Or is it malicious, containing false information that violates the platform’s review policy?

Crafting the Perfect Public Response

Your public reply is for your next guest, not your last one. Acknowledge and thank the guest for their feedback, even if it stings. If they made a valid point, state the corrective action you've taken ("We've since upgraded our Wi-Fi router to ensure seamless connectivity for future guests."). Politely correct any clear inaccuracies without being defensive, and keep it short, professional, and focused on the future.

The Limits of Platform Control

Fighting to remove a review is often a losing battle that consumes time and energy. Even if you succeed, your ranking and visibility are still subject to platform algorithms you don't control. A single algorithm change can bury your listing overnight, regardless of your 5-star streak. This is their playground; the long-term solution is to build your own.
