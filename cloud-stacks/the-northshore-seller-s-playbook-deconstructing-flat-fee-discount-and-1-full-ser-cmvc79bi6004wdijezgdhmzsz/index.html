The Commission Maze: Understanding Your Selling Options in St. Tammany Parish

Traditional 5-6% commission models have long been the standard in Louisiana real estate, but they are no longer the only choice for sellers. The landscape of the real estate market has evolved dramatically with technology, yet many commission structures remain stuck in the past. Understanding the differences is the first step toward protecting your equity.

How the Standard Commission Works (and Who Really Pays)

The conventional model typically involves a 5-6% commission on your home's final sale price. This amount is deducted directly from your proceeds at closing. It's usually split between the listing agent's brokerage (who represents you) and the buyer's agent's brokerage. For example, on a $400,000 home sale in a desirable Northshore subdivision, a 6% commission amounts to $24,000. The critical point is that you, the seller, pay the entire amount. This structure was established long before the internet, Zillow, and social media made marketing properties vastly more efficient and less expensive.

Flat-Fee MLS Services Provide a Low Entry Cost but Typically Require Northshore Sellers to Manage Their Own Work

Flat-fee services present an alluringly low upfront cost, often just a few hundred dollars. In exchange, your property gets listed on the Multiple Listing Service (MLS), the database agents use to find homes for their clients. However, this is where the service typically ends.

What You Get: The Bare Minimum

You pay a fee to get your home on the MLS, making it visible to buyer's agents. This is essentially a "For Sale By Owner" (FSBO) approach with an MLS boost. It's a digital yard sign, but not much more.

What You *Don't* Get: The Full-Service Gap

The list of what's missing is extensive and critical. With a flat-fee service, you are responsible for professional photography, creating a marketing strategy, writing compelling property descriptions, and scheduling all showings. You must also vet potential buyers, negotiate complex contracts, navigate inspection reports and repair requests, and coordinate the entire closing process. The expertise of the full-service approach pioneered by 1 Percent List HUB is designed to handle every one of these details for you.

Discount Brokerages Offer Reduced Rates but Often Achieve This by Limiting Services

Discount brokerages occupy a middle ground, offering a lower commission than the traditional 6%. However, these savings often come at a hidden cost, either through reduced services or a less personal, assembly-line approach to your sale.

The "A La Carte" Trap

Some discount real estate brokers operate on an "a la carte" model. The low advertised rate might not include essential services like professional photos, a yard sign, a lockbox for agent access, or dedicated marketing efforts. These are often add-ons that can quickly erode your initial savings.

Is Your Agent Truly Dedicated to Your Mandeville Sale?

Another common discount model involves a team-based or call-center approach. Instead of having one dedicated agent who knows you and your property, you might be passed between various specialists. This can lead to miscommunication and a lack of personal accountability, which is the last thing you need during a complex transaction like selling your home in the competitive Mandeville housing market.
